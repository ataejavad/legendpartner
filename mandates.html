Mandates

An engagement, not a subscription.

You are not purchasing a package. You are entering a private service relationship with a house that will decline the work if it cannot be done properly.

How to read this

Every engagement follows the same method. What changes between levels is the depth of the search, the reach across markets, the degree of personal access to your advisor, and how far we accompany you once introductions begin.

Fees are agreed privately and in full before any work commences. The figures below indicate where each level of engagement commences; they are not a price list, and there are no monthly charges, renewals or automatic extensions.

Which level

We will tell you honestly.

After the first consultation we recommend the level of engagement the search actually requires — including, at times, a lower one than you expected. If we do not believe we can serve you well, we say so and charge nothing.

Specificity

How defined is the brief?

Narrow requirements — faith, culture, family intentions, profession, geography — demand a wider search, not a longer list.

Geography

Where can this person be?

A single city is a defined search. Several are a campaign. No limitation at all is a mandate.

Exposure

How visible are you?

Public profile, professional sensitivity or family considerations change how a search must be conducted.

Involvement

How closely should we accompany you?

Some members want introductions and nothing more. Others want counsel through the first year.

Admission is not guaranteed at any level. We accept members we believe we can genuinely serve, and decline the rest with courtesy.
