I — Position

You retain people for your capital, your health, your affairs. For this, you have had nobody.

The market offered our members two things: an introduction service that stops at the first dinner, and a therapist who begins once something has gone wrong. Between them lies the work that actually decides the outcome — and nobody was doing it.

Legend is a private practice for that work. We establish what a partnership must be for a particular life, find it where it exists, help it establish properly, and stay with it through the years and the decisions that follow.

V — Built for this

Five things a house could not do on paper.

We built our own systems because none of what follows could be bought, and because a practice this private cannot put its members' lives inside somebody else's product. None of it decides anything. All of it is answerable to a person whose name is on the recommendation.

How judgment is supported Legend Identity — a separate service
01

A persona, built from conversation

Not a questionnaire. You talk, and what emerges is a working model of how you actually are — the things people describe badly on a form. Your advisor works from it, and you can tell it when it is wrong about you.

02

A first conversation neither of you has to have

With consent on both sides, your persona and a candidate's hold a structured exchange before anyone gives up an evening. It surfaces the divergence that would otherwise appear on the third meeting. It never decides — it hands both advisors a transcript and an argument.

03

A verification mark you can carry anywhere

A code that anyone can check against this house — on a profile, in a signature, wherever you deal with people who do not yet know you. It confirms identity and nothing else. Legend Identity is a separate service, open to anyone: most people who hold a mark are not clients of this practice, and holding one says nothing about whether you are.

04

A ledger of every disclosure

Each time we are asked whether something about you may be said, the request, your answer and the refusals are written down — permanently, and where you can read them. Most houses promise discretion. This is the version you can audit.

05

One private place for the whole engagement

Every written case, your correspondence with your advisor, your papers and your standing — held where nothing needs to travel by email. Access is issued by your advisor personally.

What none of it does: choose for you, rank anyone, or act unseen. Every candidate that reaches you was read, argued and put to you by an advisor who met them.

Correspondents

We do not hold every city.

A house that stays small cannot cover every market properly, and pretending otherwise would be the first dishonest thing we did. So in the cities where we do not have an advisor of our own, we work with a small number of people who do — houses, counsel and hosts who hold their own networks and their own standards.

If you run one of those, this page is addressed to you rather than to our members.

For family offices, private banks and lawyers whose clients raise this with them, the arrangement is different and set out separately — for professional advisors.

i

Relationship houses elsewhere

Independent matchmakers and advisory practices in markets we do not cover. Where a member's search reaches your city, we would rather work with someone who knows it than pretend we do.

ii

Relationship counsel

Therapists, mediators and family practitioners. We are not clinicians and say so; when that is the right answer we need someone qualified to send a member to, and we would like to know you before we do.

iii

Hosts and private venues

Those who run gatherings where discretion is already the standard — private rooms, clubs, collections, houses. We host a small number each season and are always short of the right rooms.

iv

Verification and security

Firms who establish that someone is who they say they are, and advise members whose visibility makes an ordinary evening complicated.

The terms, before you write

  • No fee is paid or accepted for introducing a person. Not to us, not by us. A commission would make someone a commodity, and we will not have that in the arrangement.
  • Nothing about a member reaches you without their consent, given for that occasion. The same is expected of you.
  • We do not name our correspondents publicly, and we ask that you do not name us.
  • No exclusivity, in either direction. You keep your practice and we keep ours.
  • We begin with a conversation, not a contract — and often it ends there, amicably.
