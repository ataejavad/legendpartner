Read this first

Holding a Legend mark does not mean you are a client of anything else we do.

This house is better known for its relationship practice. The mark is not part of it. It is a separate service with a separate purpose, and the two are kept apart deliberately — because a credential that implied you were looking for a partner would be useless to most of the people who need one, and unwelcome to the rest.

We do not ask why you want it. We do not offer you anything else afterwards. And a stranger checking your code is told what we verified and nothing more.

Who holds one

Anyone whose word is being taken on trust.

The problem is always the same shape: two people, no shared history, and one of them has to decide whether to believe the other. A mark does not settle everything, but it settles the first question.

i

People who meet strangers privately

Whatever the occasion — arranged through us, through someone else, or through no one at all. Most people who hold a mark did not meet us through the practice.

ii

Those who work with people they never meet

Advisors, consultants and specialists engaged remotely, where the client is asked to hand over something valuable on the strength of a profile.

iii

Private sellers and private buyers

Art, property, vehicles, anything transacted between individuals rather than through a house — where both sides would rather know who they are dealing with.

iv

Hosts, and those they admit

Private events, clubs and gatherings that would rather check a code than take a copy of a passport and become responsible for keeping it.

How to obtain one

01

Ask for an appointment

Below, or by writing to the office. We ask for your name, a city, and nothing else. There is no application to be accepted or declined — this is a service, not a membership.

02

Sit with an advisor, once

Twenty minutes, in one of our cities or wherever we can reach you. Bring an original identity document. We look at it and hand it back; we do not photograph it, copy it, or keep it.

03

Your mark, the same day

A code and a seal. You choose where to place them, and each placement gets its own code so one can be withdrawn without the others.

04

Good for a year, then seen again

In person, like the first time. A mark that is not re-confirmed lapses, and the public page says so rather than going quiet.

Fee. A single fee for the appointment and the first year, agreed before you attend and settled afterwards. Re-confirmation is charged at the same rate. There is no subscription and no charge to whoever checks your code.

What it does not do

We will not oversell this.

A mark removes one category of risk — that the person does not exist, or is not who they claim. It removes none of the others. Sold as anything more, it would make people less careful rather than safer, and we would rather have a smaller service than that.
