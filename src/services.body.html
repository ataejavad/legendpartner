II — Not on the list

Ask, and we will say whether we can.

The list above is what we do often enough to have a settled way of doing it. That is not the boundary of what can be asked. Where a request sits outside it, an advisor tells you plainly whether the house can take it, what it would involve, and what it would cost — before any of it begins.

The answer is sometimes no. A house that says yes to everything is a house that has stopped being useful about anything.
