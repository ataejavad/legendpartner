It tells you who someone is. Not what they are like.

A verified person can still be poor company, or worse. This mark removes one category of risk — that you are talking to someone who does not exist, or who is not who they say. It does not remove the others, and we will not let it be sold as though it does.

How the mark works

01

Verified in person, once

An advisor meets the person and sees an original identity document. Nothing is verified remotely, nothing from a copy, and nothing on a colleague's word. The document is handed straight back — we do not photograph or keep it.

02

A code, issued to them

Twelve characters, not derived from anything about them and not guessable from another code. They choose where to place it — a profile, a signature, a website — and each placement carries its own code so one can be withdrawn without the others.

03

They decide what it shows

The date of verification always. A first name and a city only if they have chosen to reveal them. Never a photograph, never a surname, never anything about why they came to us.

04

You check it here, not there

The seal on a profile is a picture, and pictures can be copied. The answer comes from this page or it is worth nothing. If someone shows you a seal and no code, they are showing you an image.

05

Withdrawable, instantly

By the person at any moment, and by the house where a mark is misused. A withdrawn code does not go quiet — it says so.

There is no list to browse. You cannot search this page by name, and codes cannot be enumerated. If you do not have the code, there is nothing here for you — which is the point.

Someone misusing a mark? Write to office@legendpartner.com with the code. We withdraw first and ask questions afterwards.
