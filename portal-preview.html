The case

Forty-one. Built and sold a logistics business across South-East Asia over eleven years, returned to Zurich in 2024, and now holds two board seats and a great deal more of their own time than they are used to. Divorced, no children, and direct about wanting both.

I met them first in Zurich in June and again in London last month. They are unusually unhurried for someone with that record — the kind of person who answers a question after a pause rather than during it. They read seriously. They cook. They have kept the same three friends since university, which I have learned to treat as meaningful.

On the point you raised in March: they have moved for a person before and would do so again, but they said it without the eagerness of someone performing flexibility.

Why I am bringing this to you

You have twice told me that you do not want to be the more ambitious person in the room, and twice declined people who agreed with you too readily. This is neither.

The practical fit is also better than it looks on paper: Zurich and London is a ninety-minute problem, not a transatlantic one, and their calendar is now their own in a way that yours is not.

Where I am less certain

They came out of a long marriage two years ago and have met very few people since. I do not think that is a reason to wait, but you should hear it from me first rather than discover it over dinner.

What remains withheld

Their name, employer, photograph and city of residence are withheld until you accept. They have not been told your identity, your profession, or that this case exists. Nothing is disclosed on either side without consent given for this introduction specifically.

Counsel

Work on a relationship while you are inside it. Sessions with someone retained by you, separately or together — for a difficulty that has stopped resolving itself, or simply to keep a good thing durable.

Status

Available — not currently running

Nothing is running here. Counsel begins when you ask for it, and it can be asked for at any point — including years after we last spoke.

It is not a course with a fixed number of sessions, and it is not something we will suggest you need. You decide when it starts and when it stops.

If it is for both, we ask each of you separately. Either may decline, and the other is not told which.

What counsel covers

01

A difficulty that has stopped moving

The one you have each raised twice and put down twice. Almost no relationship ends suddenly — it ends after a long conversation neither person could start.

02

The argument that recurs

Usually because it was never about what it appeared to be about. Worth naming properly, once, with someone in the room who is not either of you.

03

Durability

What this relationship actually needs in order to still be here in ten years — asked while things are good, which is the only time the answer is honest.

04

A decision taken jointly

Money, geography, children, two sets of family, a career that moves one of you. Decisions that are hard because they are shared, not because they are complicated.

05

Whether it should continue

Sometimes the honest counsel is that it should not. We will say so, and we will handle the ending with the same discretion as the beginning.
