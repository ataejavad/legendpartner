Services

Everything the house does.

One list, in one place. Retained singly or held together under a mandate — and where something you want is not on it, say so and we will tell you honestly whether we can.

I — The list

What can be retained.

Each of these is retained on its own terms. Nothing here obliges you to take anything else, and a member who wants one thing and nothing else is an ordinary member.

What each costs Request a consultation
01

Search & introduction

A search run for you across all four tracks, and a written case for each person proposed. It does not include a list to browse, and it does not include being shown someone we have not met.

02

Events & companionship

Parties, events, business events, travel and the social occasions where arriving alone is the difficult part — with the terms agreed in writing beforehand rather than improvised on the night.

03

Relationship management

Formation for the first year, counsel when something has stopped resolving itself, and continuity across the decades. None of it requires that we introduced you.

II — Not on the list

Ask, and we will say whether we can.

The list above is what we do often enough to have a settled way of doing it. That is not the boundary of what can be asked. Where a request sits outside it, an advisor tells you plainly whether the house can take it, what it would involve, and what it would cost — before any of it begins.

The answer is sometimes no. A house that says yes to everything is a house that has stopped being useful about anything.
